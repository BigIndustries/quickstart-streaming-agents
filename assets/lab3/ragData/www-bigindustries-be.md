Matthias Vallaey

Matthias is founder of Big Industries He has a strong track record in the IT-
Services and Software Industry, working across many verticals. He is highly

that exceeds customer expectations. In his role as Entrepreneur he is building
partnerships with IT Vendors and introduces their technology where they bring

![Sandeep-Singh](https://www.bigindustries.be/hubfs/popular-

Sandeep Singh

Sandeep is technical lead and a highly motivated, enthusiastic and competent
Big Data Architect who has gained extensive experience architecting,
implementing and operating large scale data processing solutions based on
enterprise and emergent Big Data technologies  including designing,
implementing and integrating solutions with information security & IT
governance systems.

![Joris-Billen](https://www.bigindustries.be/hubfs/popular-
bigindustries/team/Joris-Billen.png)

Joris Billen

Joris is a computational engineer with a strong background in Applied Physics
(Civil Engineer) and Data Engineering.

Joris holds a Master Degree in Applied Physics Engineering and a PhD in
Computational Science. At Big Industries he found an environment that fosters
productive, collaborative teamwork to further expand his Software Engineering
expertise.

![Srivatsan-Sadagopan](https://www.bigindustries.be/hubfs/popular-
bigindustries/team/Srivatsan-Sadagopan.png)

Srivatsan Sadagopan

Srivatsan has a Master of Science in Software Systems and Data Warehousing. He
has a strong domain knowledge in Telecommunications and Financial Services.
His passion for software development helped him to evolve his career from pure
software testing to a lead full stack developer role. He is a Big Data
engineer with experience in developing data flow pipelines from data
collection and processing to the production of data insights. In the recent
years, his expertise is in developing Apache Kafka, Spark and Flink based
applications, engineering solutions using IBM cloud for one of the largest
commercial banks in Belgium.
